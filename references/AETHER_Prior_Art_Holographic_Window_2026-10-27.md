# Prior art: eye-tracked holographic windows (checked 27 Oct 2026)

Context: AETHER v6 main line = eye-tracked true holographic window (card + TV size). Who already does this:

- SeeReal Technologies (Germany): RGB laser backlight + LC SLM + real-time eye tracking + beam-steered viewing windows; sub-holograms per scene point. Same architecture as AETHER v6. Many patents; no shipping product found. https://seereal.com/how-does-it-work-seereals-holographic-display-principle-in-a-nutshell
- Samsung SAIT, "Slim-panel holographic video display", Nature Communications, Nov 2020: ~1 cm thick, steering backlight with LC beam deflector, 15 deg view up to 1 m; prototype, must get thinner for phones. https://techxplore.com/news/2020-11-samsung-slim-panel-holographic-video.html
- VividQ (UK): real-time CGH software; automotive HUD, AR, planned laptop-scale holographic display on standard LCD panels (2021 funding news). https://www.eenewseurope.com/en/vividq-raises-11m-for-holographic-displays/
- Swave Photonics (Belgium): phase-change-material holographic SLM, smallest pixels, for AR glasses; CES 2026 Innovation honoree; still in development. https://www.gophotonics.com/news/details/8171-swave-photonics-set-to-be-honored-at-ces-2026-for-its-holographic-spatial-light-modulator
- Google Beam (ex-Project Starline): life-size 3D video calls, 6 cameras + AI 3D + light-field display, head tracking, shipping to enterprises from late 2025. Not holography (no true focus cues). https://techcrunch.com/2025/05/20/googles-3d-teleconferencing-platform-now-called-beam-will-ship-later-in-2025

Implications: physics proven by prototypes; core eye-tracked-window concept is NOT AETHER's invention (SeeReal patents may block); nobody ships a product (shared bottlenecks: fast phase LC, cost, large panels). Possible AETHER position: pocket card window + home TV window, true holography for life-size family calls. Novelty unverified until a formal patent search.
