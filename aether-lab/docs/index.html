The AI is the engineer.
OpenCascade is the instrument.

AETHER Lab turns a three-view drawing into parametric OpenCascade B-rep CAD and checks it against every view of the drawing. You then keep engineering by chat: refine it, cut a section, add a real B-rep pocket, export STEP. Every change is a recorded feature, and you can undo it even after a restart.

How it works

1 · MeasureComputer vision on the ink: the views, silhouettes and sections. No model is involved.
2 · UnderstandMuse Spark, Muse Wonder and SAM 3.1 label parts and regions. Answers are schema-checked JSON, stored, never executed.
3 · BuildA parametric .aethercad document recomputes into OpenCascade B-rep. Every value is tagged measured / inferred / assumed.
4 · VerifyThe B-rep is projected back into every view, every solid passes BRepCheck, and STEP is re-read in a fresh process.
5 · ConverseMuse Spark runs each chat turn as a tool loop over the same document: refine, section, pocket, export, undo.
