One paper drawing in.

AETHER Lab reads a single page of a mechanical drawing — a century-old patent figure with no scale and no second view — and Meta's models turn it into a 3D assembly whose gears mesh, explain it part by part, and check how sharp it would look on a holographic workbench. The workbench is simulated, not built.

How it works

1 · Muse Spark readsThe whole sheet and four close-ups, each twice; a part is kept only when both readings agree or the drawing confirms it.
2 · Muse Wonder completesDepth, planes, the hidden shafts and bearings, which gears mesh — each added part with its engineering reason.
3 · The engine rebuildsInvolute gears that mesh, pins through the stack, plates with their holes; GLB and STL export.
4 · Muse Spark explainsStep by step, highlighting each part; every part id and number is checked against the model in code.
5 · The desk checksA wave-optics eye model scores every part on the AETHER Desk (simulation).
